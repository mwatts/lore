R4 Lore track: the fix round, measured

The seed regression at 50,000 Records was a layout bug in the index
journal: a Lore repository is created and closed before its first fold,
and the next open read its bucket-less index as a pre-fan-out store, so
every group sat at 256 buckets and each fold rewrote about 15,000 bucket
files. Lore f62f840 opens such a store as new. The seed at 50,000 now
writes 72.1 KB per Record (122.3 before the fix, 82.0 with change 2
alone), and a create writes about 0.4 MB with its share of the folds
(0.9 to 1.16 MB before). Lore 7b476ed stamps a journaled store's info
file so Lore 24545866 refuses it instead of losing the journal; a
scripted run with both binaries shows the refusal, and the silent loss
without the stamp.

The failed reopen after the change 2 seed was the projection's SurrealKV
lock, not Lore. It did not recur in five runs at 50,000, one under heavy
load; the doc gives the inferred cause in cairn-surreal's close bound.
The probe now reopens at once, reports a failed attempt, times the close,
and runs the optional write attributor over the seed too.
